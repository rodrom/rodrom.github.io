Contract options

Online and in-person in Berlin

I generally work remotely, but I’m happy to join teams or projects and attend meetings in person in Berlin or the surrounding area. Occasional trips to other German cities or even elsewhere in Europe are also no problem, provided they are planned in advance.

Registered office and billing address

My business is registered in Germany and I live in Berlin. I can issue invoices throughout Europe (I am registered with the VIES directory) and internationally without any issues.

Languages

I am fluent in Spanish, as it is my mother tongue. I have a professional level of English. I understand Italian and speak it quite fluently. And I know enough German to understand what I read and what people say to me, although my speaking and writing skills are a little weaker, but I hope to improve them soon.

Pricing

My hourly rate depends on the scope, complexity, and urgency of the project. In most cases, it ranges between €45 and €60 per hour (net). For long-term collaborations or projects, any cost calculation methodology that works well for both parties is suitable.
