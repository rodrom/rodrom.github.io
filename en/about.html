About me

My name is Rodrigo Román García, or rodrom. I hold a degree in Computer Engineering from the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid and have over 20 years’ experience in information technology. I have been living in Berlin since 2016 and have been working as a freelancer since 2020, offering my services to small businesses specialising in online and face-to-face training for psychologists in EMDR. I have also worked as a software developer for companies of various sizes in the online retail sector.

My knowledge and experience cover a wide range of areas. I primarily develop software solutions across all phases of the software lifecycle, from conception through to maintenance, including design, implementation and testing. I tailor the results and budgets to my clients’ needs. But the best thing is to take a look at my projects or professional services. Still doubts, contact me!.
